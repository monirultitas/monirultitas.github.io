What I study
Research Interests

My work sits at the intersection of earth observation, machine learning and field-based environmental science in data-scarce, climate-exposed regions.

🛰️
Flood Susceptibility Mapping
SAR (Sentinel-1), optical (Sentinel-2), terrain and rainfall integration using RF and SVM in GEE.
🌊
Haor & Wetland Hydrology
Flash flood dynamics, LULC change and disaster risk in Bangladesh's northeastern haor basin.
🤖
GeoAI & Deep Learning
Applying geospatial machine learning to climate risk modelling and environmental monitoring.
🚁
UAV Photogrammetry
Low-altitude drone mapping for informal settlements, agricultural surveys and disaster response.
🌿
Water Quality & Pollution
Satellite-based monitoring of eutrophication and organic pollution in peri-urban river systems.
📡
Low-Cost Environmental Sensing
IoT weather stations and Arduino sensors to densify microclimate data in underserved areas.
