Monirul Islam

Remote Sensing & GIS Researcher  ·  PhD Aspirant  ·  Dhaka, Bangladesh

I study how environmental hazards — floods, climate extremes, water pollution — concentrate risk in data-scarce places and how that risk reaches people's health and livelihoods. My work combines satellite remote sensing and machine learning (Google Earth Engine, Python) with low-cost field sensing (UAV/drone mapping, IoT weather stations) and community-led data collection.

Current projects: flood susceptibility mapping in the Haor region using SAR and ML; climate-dengue risk in Dhaka's informal settlements; satellite water-quality monitoring of the Buriganga River; and UAV agricultural surveys in Bogura.
