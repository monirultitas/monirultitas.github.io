What I study

Research Interests

Earth observation, machine learning and field science applied to flood risk, water quality and climate vulnerability in data-scarce regions.

🛰️
Flood Susceptibility Mapping
SAR (Sentinel-1), optical (Sentinel-2), terrain and rainfall using Random Forest and SVM in Google Earth Engine.
🌊
Haor & Wetland Hydrology
Flash flood dynamics, LULC change and disaster risk in Bangladesh's northeastern haor basin.
🤖
GeoAI & Deep Learning
Geospatial machine learning for climate risk modelling and large-scale environmental monitoring.
🚁
UAV Photogrammetry
Low-altitude drone mapping for informal settlements, agriculture and disaster response.
🌿
Water Quality Monitoring
Satellite-based eutrophication and organic pollution tracking in peri-urban river systems.
📡
Low-Cost Environmental Sensing
IoT weather stations and Arduino sensors to fill microclimate data gaps in underserved communities.
"Mapping risk where data runs out."
